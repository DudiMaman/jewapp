# וְאָהַבְתָּ · Ve'ahavta

A daily Jewish-life companion for **prayer, Tehillim (Psalms), and mitzvot** — with an AI
guide and gentle, Shabbat-aware reminders. Subscription mobile app for **iOS + Android**,
Hebrew-first (RTL). The name is drawn from the Shema — *"וְאָהַבְתָּ אֵת ה׳ אֱלֹהֶיךָ"*.

## At a glance

- **Default language: Hebrew (RTL)**; language is changeable in the personal area (Settings)
- **5 tabs:** שיחה (Chat) · סידור (Siddur) · היום (Today) · תהילים (Tehillim) · תנ"ך (Tanakh)
- **Personalized** by nusach, gender, age, Hebrew name, and Hebrew reading level
- **A companion** that explains any prayer/verse and recommends the right chapter
- **Daily journey** with streaks that respect Shabbat, and time-aware push reminders
- **Subscription:** 7-day free trial · ₪17.90/week · ₪149.90/year
